# Case Study: Business Analytics

## Problem

Retail teams need to understand revenue trends, customer value, product performance, retention, and product associations.

## Dataset

Synthetic retail workbook with order, product, customer, and inventory data.

## Approach

Use pandas modules for repeatable analytics and Streamlit/Plotly for interactive exploration.

## Key Techniques

- groupby aggregation
- merges across orders and order items
- time series resampling
- RFM segmentation
- cohort retention matrix
- ABC classification
- market basket rule metrics

## Output

- KPI cards
- revenue trend
- RFM summary
- cohort heatmap and cohort size
- ABC mix
- market basket rules
